Routing

Patchbay behaviour & the tape workflow

1 · What a patchbay column does

Every column is two jacks wired to two different things. The top jack is hardwired to a source's output. The bottom jack is hardwired to a destination's input. The normal is an internal link between them that a cable can break.

Grey = the normalling jack (the NYS-SPP-L1 ships with it on the bottom). Orange = a patch cable you insert.
Take from a top jack — costs nothing.
Give to a bottom jack — overrides the default.

2 · The tape workflow

Two passes with a gap between them. Nothing is real-time: you print, then you play back and capture.

Commit the sound on the 424. Decide the arrangement in Logic.
The thing you can't undo is the thing you shouldn't want to.

3 · Which elements go to tape

Four tracks, so you choose. Printing everything flattens the record — the contrast between tape-coloured and clean is what makes the colour read.

Usual picks: RD-8 drums (an 808 through tape is the canonical sound), bass, one pad or the VC-340, guitar that's already been through the pedals.
Usually leave clean: anything where detail matters more than weight.
