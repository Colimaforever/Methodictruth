Learn Production

From a raw idea to a finished mix — seen, not just described

Making a record is really four crafts, in order: capture the sound, arrange it into a song, mix it so it hits, then master it for the world. Most tutorials bury this under jargon. These four free guides draw it instead — polar patterns, an energy arc, an EQ curve, a loudness meter — with the exact Logic Pro X steps to do each. Read them in order, or jump to what you need.

Part 1 · Capture 🎙Recording get the sound in

A great mix is mostly decided before a plugin opens — at the mic. What each microphone is for, what a polar pattern actually looks like, how two inches of placement changes the tone, stereo technique, reamping, how compression works, and how to punch in and comp a flawless take.
