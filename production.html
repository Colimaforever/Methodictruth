Learn Production

From a raw idea to a finished mix — seen, not just described

Making a record is really five crafts, in order: capture the sound, arrange it into a song, learn what the effects do, mix it so it hits, then master it for the world. Most tutorials bury this under jargon. These free guides draw it instead — polar patterns, an energy arc, an EQ curve, a loudness meter — with the exact Logic Pro X steps to do each. Read them in order, or jump to what you need. Below the path there are four craft guides that don't belong to any single stage but decide more than all of them: choosing sounds, rhythm, groove, and actually finishing.

The Craft Guides

What decides more than any of the five

These three don't belong to a single stage of the path — they run underneath all of it. They're also, in honest order of impact, probably where the biggest gains are: the sound you choose determines how much mixing you'll have to do, rhythm and groove determine whether anyone feels the track at all, and finishing determines whether any of it ever gets heard.
