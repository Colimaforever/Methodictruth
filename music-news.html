𒌓 The Signal, Not the Noise

Music technology moves in three currents at once, and this feed carries all of them. Audio research (arXiv preprints on sound, audio processing, and machine listening) tells you what's coming for tools like our Song Analyzer years before it ships. Gear & software (synth releases, free plugins, DAW updates) tells you what you can make music with today — a shocking amount of it free. Production & analysis (the independent music-tech press) tells you how working musicians actually use it all.

Use the search to follow one thread across every source — a synth you're eyeing, a technique, "free plugin" is a perennially rewarding query. Toggle sources off if one is drowning the rest; your choices are remembered. And when something here inspires a sound, the Synth is one tab away.

Dispatches are cached for 20 minutes to keep things fast and gentle on the sources — hit Refresh for the bleeding edge.
