# What survived in Drive

The mini was erased in October 2026 with no backup and no FileVault recovery
key. This is the inventory of what was already in Google Drive when that
happened — an accident of ad-hoc uploads rather than a backup policy, and the
only reason the catalogue still exists.

**These are real Logic projects, not bounces.** A `.logicx` is a folder that
macOS displays as a single file; uploaded to Drive it becomes an ordinary
folder, which is why each one here contains Logic's own `Project File Backups`.
To use one again: download the folder, rename it `Name.logicx` on a Mac, and it
opens as a project.

Most of these live in the Drive folder **`logic stems`**; the 2025 ones are
under **`Logic`**. Dates are each project's own last-modified time, not the
upload date.

## What to check first

1. **Open one project and play it** before trusting the set. Greyed-out regions
   mean Logic is looking for audio that lived outside the package.
2. `sslsixpreset.logicx` is worth opening early — by its name it holds console
   settings, which would otherwise be a slow rediscovery.
3. The gap is **2025 onward**. Four projects from January 2025 and nothing
   after. Anything written in the twenty months before the erase existed only
   on the mini.
